## NASA Space App Challenge 2026
The NASA International Space Apps Challenge 2026 is a global 48-hour hackathon where innovators, coders, designers, and space enthusiasts collaborate to solve real-world problems on Earth and in space. Using NASA’s free and open data, participants build creative prototypes, apps, and visualizations under the 2026 theme, "The Next Frontier."

### Space Mission Design Game
Our project is a Space Mission Design and Launch Simulator that lets players plan and control a space mission. Players can customise spacecraft, prepare rockets for launch, perform safety checks, and complete space mission objectives. We created this game to make young astro-enthusiasts learning about space and Space Mission design more interactive. We made effective use of several languages like Three.JS, HTML, CSS, and Super-Intelligence (Sonnet 5.5, Gemini Argon) for problem-solving and debugging code errors, as well as mentors who helped us by brainstorming and research throughout the project. We considered the Mission design hurdles that come during the launch of the spacecraft, such as telemetry, air, and gravity behaviour.
