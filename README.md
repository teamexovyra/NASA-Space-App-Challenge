My project is a Space Mission Design and Launch Simulator that allows players to experience planning and controlling a space mission. Players can customise spacecraft, prepare rockets for launch, perform safety checks and complete space mission objectives. I created this game to make learning about space and technology more interactive while also improving my coding, creativity, effective use of ai and problem-solving skills.
